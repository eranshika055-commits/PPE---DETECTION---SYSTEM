Construction Safety

Safety Guide

Basic PPE practices for safer construction sites.

🪖

Hard Hat Safety

  • Wear a properly fitted hard hat.
  • Check the helmet before use.
  • Replace damaged helmets.
  • Keep the helmet correctly adjusted.
😷

Mask Safety

  • Use appropriate respiratory protection.
  • Make sure the mask fits correctly.
  • Replace damaged or contaminated masks.
  • Follow site-specific safety procedures.
🦺

Safety Vest

  • Wear a high-visibility safety vest.
  • Keep reflective areas visible.
  • Replace damaged safety vests.
  • Wear required PPE in designated areas.
🚧

Construction Site Safety

  • Follow site safety instructions.
  • Stay within designated work areas.
  • Be aware of machinery and vehicles.
  • Report unsafe conditions.
Project Information

About the Project

An AI-based system designed to assist construction-site PPE monitoring.

Project Objective

The PPE Safety Detection System uses computer vision to identify Personal Protective Equipment in construction-site images and videos.

The system can identify protective equipment such as hard hats, masks and safety vests, as well as situations where required PPE is missing.

Technology Used

Python YOLOv8 OpenCV Flask HTML CSS JavaScript
